About the museum

Their missions ended. Their discoveries did not.

Beyond Their Mission is a space museum about the machines that travelled beyond Earth to explore the Moon, Mars and interstellar space. Some were left behind after finishing their work. Some fell silent after years of exploration. Others are still sending discoveries home.

Purpose

To show why NASA sent each machine, how its engineering worked, what it discovered and what happened next.

Who it is for

Students, families and anyone curious about space. Everything is written to be easy to read.

Educational goals

Understand how space machines work, how scientists use evidence, and the difference between what a finding shows and what it does not prove.

Not an official NASA site

This is an independent educational project. It uses NASA mission facts and links to NASA sources. Check NASA pages for the latest status.
