Exhibition 1

What we left behind

Bringing hardware home costs too much fuel and weight, so it stays. Here is everything NASA has left on the Moon and Mars, and how each piece ended up there.

Why it was left behind

Carrying hardware home would cost far too much fuel and weight. On the Moon there is no air or weather, so equipment survives for a very long time, but sunlight, huge temperature swings and tiny meteorites slowly wear it down. NASA has published guidelines asking future missions to protect the Apollo landing sites.

Correction: an earlier list said Mars Polar Lander had been found in orbital pictures. A 2005 image showed a possible match, but it was never confirmed, so its fate is officially unknown.
